🧠 Learn & Explore

What is Shear Modulus?

Shear modulus, also called modulus of rigidity, measures how strongly a solid resists a change in shape when a tangential force acts on it.

G = τ / γ = (F / A) / (Δx / h)

τ = Shear stress
γ = Shear strain
F = Applied force
A = Area of the face
Δx = Sideways displacement
h = Height of the block

📈 Shear Stress vs Shear Strain

The slope of the stress-strain graph in the elastic region represents the shear modulus.

🎯 Experiment Details

Objective

To understand how a tangential force changes the shape of a solid and how shear modulus is calculated.

Procedure

  1. Select a material.
  2. Set the force and dimensions.
  3. Click Apply Force.
  4. Observe the block deformation.
  5. Read the calculated values.
  6. Record readings and compare materials.

Conclusion

For a given shear stress, a material with a larger shear modulus undergoes less shear strain.
