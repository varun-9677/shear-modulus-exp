🧠 Learn & Explore

What is Shear Modulus?

Shear modulus, also called modulus of rigidity, measures how strongly a solid resists a change in shape caused by a tangential force.

G = Shear Stress / Shear Strain

Shear stress = F / A

Shear strain = sideways displacement / height

Unit of G: Pascal (Pa)

📈 Shear Stress vs Shear Strain

The slope of the straight-line graph in the elastic region is the shear modulus.

🎯 Experiment Details

Objective

To understand how a tangential force changes the shape of a solid and how shear modulus is calculated.

Procedure

  1. Select a material.
  2. Set force and block dimensions.
  3. Click Apply Force.
  4. Observe the deformation and calculated values.
  5. Record readings and compare materials.

Conclusion

For a given shear stress, a material with a larger shear modulus undergoes less shear strain.
